Atizzy Account Deletion

Last updated: August 26, 2026 · Version 1.0

You can request deletion of your Atizzy account and associated personal data. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete from the Atizzy app

1. Sign in to Atizzy and open your profile or account settings.
2. Open Security and choose Delete my account.
3. Confirm the permanent deletion request when prompted.

The in-app request signs out the account and sends the deletion operation through Atizzy’s authenticated account-deletion flow.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email olaideheritagetemitope@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Atizzy account. Include “Atizzy account deletion request” in the subject. We may request reasonable information to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What is deleted

We delete or de-identify the account profile and personal information associated with the verified request, subject to technical and legal limits. User-uploaded profile and media content is removed where applicable. Some records, such as payment, ticket, fraud-prevention, security, audit, or dispute records, may be retained for the period required to complete transactions, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable obligations; retained records are restricted and are not used to keep the account active.

Processing time

Requests are handled after ownership verification. Some systems may require additional time to complete deletion, backups may expire later, and content already shared publicly may have been copied by others before deletion.
