Atizzy Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026 · Version 1.0

Atizzy helps people discover events, artists, music, venues, playlists, and tickets. This policy explains what information the service uses and why.

Information we use

When you create or use an Atizzy account, we may process your email address, authentication identifiers, profile information, roles, preferences, and account activity. If you use discovery features, we may process device location when you grant permission so that nearby events and venues can be calculated. Location permission is optional.

Atizzy may also process content that you choose to provide, including profile images, event and venue images, music, videos, artwork, lyrics, playlists, comments, ratings, and other user-generated content. Ticket orders, payment status, transaction references, premium or verification entitlements, and wallet/order records are processed to deliver paid services and prevent duplicate or unauthorized transactions.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, maintain profiles and role-based workspaces, provide event and music discovery, calculate nearby results, publish user-provided content, process ticket and subscription transactions, issue and manage tickets, provide support, prevent abuse, keep records accurate, and improve reliability and security.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be transmitted to infrastructure and service providers that support authentication, database and file storage, maps and geocoding, payment processing, and ticket or notification delivery. Payment card credentials are handled by the payment provider; Atizzy uses provider responses and transaction references to reconcile orders and entitlements. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

Atizzy uses encrypted HTTPS connections for data in transit and applies authenticated access controls to account and role-based data. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain transaction and safety records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. Published or public user content may remain visible until it is removed by an authorized user or administrator.

Your choices and deletion

You may update available profile information, revoke location permission through your device settings, remove content where the app provides that control, and request account deletion. Visit Account deletion for the available deletion process. You may also contact olaideheritagetemitope@gmail.com.

Children

Atizzy is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date and version at the top of this page identify the current publication.

Contact
